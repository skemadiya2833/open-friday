# Make Time (Knapp & Zeratsky)

**Attribution:** Jake Knapp & John Zeratsky — *Make Time* (paraphrased)  
**Evidence:** heuristic

- Pick one highlight for the day — the thing you'd be glad you did.
- Laser mode: block distractions during the highlight window.
- Energize basics: movement, food, sleep — boring but load-bearing.
- Reflect with a short daily note: what worked, what to tweak.
- Default calendars and feeds are designed to steal attention — redesign defaults.
- Batch reactive work; don't let the inbox set the highlight.
