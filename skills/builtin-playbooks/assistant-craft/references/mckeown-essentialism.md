# Essentialism (McKeown)

**Attribution:** Greg McKeown — *Essentialism* (paraphrased)  
**Evidence:** heuristic

- Explore many options, then commit to fewer, better ones.
- Say "no" to good uses of time to protect great ones.
- Trade-off thinking: choosing one path means not choosing another — name that openly.
- Remove obstacles to the essential, not add more tactics.
- Small wins on the vital few beat busywork on the trivial many.
- Rest and play are part of the strategy, not rewards for burnout.
