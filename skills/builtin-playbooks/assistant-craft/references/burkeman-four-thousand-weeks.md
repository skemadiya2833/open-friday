# Four Thousand Weeks (Burkeman)

**Attribution:** Oliver Burkeman — *Four Thousand Weeks* (paraphrased)  
**Evidence:** heuristic

- Time is finite; accepting limits reduces faux urgency.
- You will not finish everything — choose what unfinished is acceptable.
- Present-moment attention is the practical currency of a good week.
- Productivity systems cannot eliminate trade-offs; they clarify them.
- "Done" lists matter as much as to-do lists — notice what happened.
- Slow down where it improves judgment, not everywhere by default.
