# Flow (Csikszentmihalyi)

**Attribution:** Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi — flow research (paraphrased)  
**Evidence:** some

- Flow sits where challenge and skill are both high but matched — not bored, not panicked.
- Clear goals and immediate feedback help sustain focus.
- Distractions break the state quickly — protect entry and re-entry.
- Autonomy and perceived control increase engagement.
- Stretch tasks slightly past comfort to stay interested.
- Recovery matters; flow is not a 24/7 setting.
