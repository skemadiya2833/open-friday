# Implementation intentions

**Attribution:** Gollwitzer & research on if-then planning (paraphrased)  
**Evidence:** strong

- "When situation X, I will do Y" beats vague goals for follow-through.
- Tie actions to cues you will actually encounter (time, place, preceding event).
- Pre-decide the next step to reduce friction at the moment of choice.
- Keep plans specific but flexible if the cue fails — replan, don't guilt.
- One intention per cue avoids overload.
- Review intentions when context changes (travel, new job, new calendar).
